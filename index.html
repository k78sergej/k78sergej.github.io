About me.

I run a heavily customized Hyprland desktop on Arch, keep a couple of always-on servers humming for backups and small services, and would rather repurpose an old laptop than buy something new. Most projects here started the same way: I wanted a specific tool that didn't exist yet, so I built it, tested it against my own real setup, and just kept using it.
